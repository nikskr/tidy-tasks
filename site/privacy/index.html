Tidy Tasks

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026 · Publisher: mik_dev · Contact: mgpmessage@gmail.com

Summary. Tidy Tasks does not require an account and does not collect your tasks. Your tasks are stored only on your device.

What we collect

Crash and performance diagnostics. If the app crashes or runs slowly, it sends a technical report to our error-monitoring provider (Sentry). A report contains the app version, device model, operating system version, the technical error and the screens visited before it. It does not contain the text of your tasks, your name, email, contacts, location or advertising identifiers. Reports are not linked to your identity and are not used for advertising or tracking.

Purpose: finding and fixing bugs (legitimate interest). Retention: up to 90 days.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your data.

Your data on your device

Tasks are stored in a local database on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. We cannot access or recover them.

Service providers

Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) — crash reporting. Data is sent encrypted over HTTPS.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

We will update this page and the “Last updated” date. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
