Frequently asked

Where are my tasks stored?
Only on your device. There is no account or cloud copy.

Can I move my tasks to a new phone?
Not yet. Device-to-device sync is planned for a future version.

I deleted the app — can I get my tasks back?
No. Deleting the app deletes its data, and we never had a copy.
