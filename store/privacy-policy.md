## Summary
Tidy Tasks does not require an account and does not collect your tasks. Your tasks are stored only on your device.

## What we collect
**Crash and performance diagnostics.** If the app crashes or runs slowly, it sends a technical report to our
error-monitoring provider (Sentry). A report contains: app version, device model, operating system version,
the technical error and the screens visited before it. It does **not** contain the text of your tasks, your name,
email, contacts, location or advertising identifiers. Reports are not linked to your identity and are not used for
advertising or tracking.

Purpose: to find and fix bugs (legitimate interest). Retention: up to 90 days.

## What we do not do
- No accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no selling or sharing of data.

## Your data on your device
Tasks are stored in a local database on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. We cannot access or recover them.

## Processors
Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) — crash reporting. Data is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS).

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

## Changes
We will update this page and the "Last updated" date. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
